HOW THE CALCULATOR WORKS

How are rewards calculated?

Every 1,000 dishes available to the kitchen gives 5 Service Coins and 5 Azure. Dishes used to feed farm animals are removed from kitchen production. 10,000 Azure equals $1.

How does Azure reinvestment work?

When enabled, Azure is treated as reinvested into Service Coins at a 10% bonus: 1 Azure becomes 1.1 Service Coins. This is used when calculating the time to reach the next chef.

How do ad rewards work?

Each ad gives 10 Service Coins instantly, up to 10 ads (100 Service Coins) per day. The calculator assumes the selected ads are watched immediately, and for longer estimates adds the same bonus again every 24 hours.
