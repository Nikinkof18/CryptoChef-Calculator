HOW THE CALCULATOR WORKS

How are rewards calculated?

Every 1,000 dishes sold gives 5 Service Coins and 5 Azure. 10,000 Azure equals $1.

How does Azure conversion work?

Azure can be converted into Service Coins with a 10% bonus: 1 Azure becomes 1.1 Service Coins.

What does “Best Action” mean?

ChefCalc compares your next chef purchases using cost, production gain and payback time, while respecting your Azure-conversion and withdrawal preference.

ChefCalc is an independent community calculator. Game values can be changed in data.js.
