HOW THE CALCULATOR WORKS

How are rewards calculated?

Every 1,000 dishes sold gives 5 Service Coins and 5 Azure. 10,000 Azure equals $1.

How does Azure reinvestment work?

When enabled, Azure is treated as reinvested into Service Coins at a 10% bonus: 1 Azure becomes 1.1 Service Coins. This is used when calculating the time to reach the next chef.

How do ad rewards work?

Each ad gives 25 Service Coins immediately, up to 10 ads (250 Service Coins) per day. The future-time estimate uses the daily ad bonus as an average hourly rate; the exact result depends on when ads are watched and when the daily limit resets.

ChefCalc is an independent community calculator. Game values can be changed in data.js.
